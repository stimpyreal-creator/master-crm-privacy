# Privacy Policy for MASTER CRM

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **MASTER CRM** ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide cloud synchronization and account management features, we collect and store the following data:
* **Account Information:** Your email address and encrypted authentication credentials used to create and access your account.
* **User-Generated Content (CRM Data):** Service orders, client addresses, phone numbers, repair notes, spare parts lists, and financial calculations that you voluntarily enter into the App.
* **Device Security Data:** Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face unlock) is processed locally on your device via the Android Biometric API. We do not collect, transmit, or store your biometric data on our servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely to:
* Authenticate your access to your personal workspace.
* Synchronize and back up your CRM records across your devices via secure cloud storage (Supabase).
* Provide password recovery functionality upon your request.

We **do not** sell, rent, or share your personal data or your clients' records with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored locally on your device and synchronized with cloud servers provided by **Supabase** using encrypted HTTPS connections and Row Level Security (RLS), ensuring that only you can access your records.

## 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion
You have full control over your data:
* **Export & Backup:** You can export your data locally at any time using the built-in backup feature.
* **Account Deletion:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud and local data directly inside the App by navigating to **Settings → Delete Account and All Data**. Once confirmed, all records are immediately and irreversibly erased from our servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The App uses **Supabase** for authentication and database hosting. Please refer to Supabase's Privacy Policy for information on how they secure infrastructure.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## Account and Data Deletion Instructions (Удаление аккаунта и данных)

Users of **MASTER CRM** can permanently delete their account and all associated cloud and local data at any time using either of the following methods:

### Method 1: Instant Deletion Inside the App
1. Open **MASTER CRM** on your device and sign in to your account.
2. Long-press the **«ДЕТАЛИ» (PARTS)** button in the top navigation bar to open **Settings**.
3. Scroll down to the account section and tap **«Удалить аккаунт и все данные» (Delete Account and All Data)**.
4. Confirm the deletion. All your profile credentials, service orders, client records, and financial calculations will be **immediately and permanently erased** from our Supabase cloud servers and your device.

### Method 2: Request Deletion via Email
If you no longer have access to the app, you can request manual deletion by sending an email to **[master.crm13@gmail.com]** with the subject **"Delete MASTER CRM Account"** from the email address registered in the app. Your account and all associated data will be completely deleted within **48 hours**.
